CardScan

Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

CardScan ("the app") is an iPhone app made by Andrew Stevens ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short, we do not collect, store, sell or share your personal information. CardScan has no servers and no accounts of its own.

What the app handles

  • Photos and screenshots of business cards and email signatures that you take or choose. These are read entirely on your iPhone using Apple's built-in text recognition. They are not uploaded anywhere. A copy is kept in the app's Card Library on your iPhone (see below) until you delete it.
  • Pasted text such as an email signature you copy into the app. It is read on your iPhone in the same way.
  • The contact details read from them: names, job titles, companies, phone numbers, email addresses, websites, postal addresses, and a cropped photo or logo if you have turned that on. These belong to the people whose cards you scan.
  • Your Contacts. With your permission, the app adds new contacts to your iPhone's Contacts. Before saving, it looks up your existing contacts by email address, phone number, or name and company, only to check whether the person is already there. This check happens on your iPhone, and your existing contacts are never uploaded or shared.

Card Library

Each card or signature you save is kept in CardScan's Card Library: the card photo, the details read from it, the date, and where it was saved. The library is stored only in the app's own storage on your iPhone, using your iPhone's storage. It is included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup, which is your own. It is never sent to us or to any server we operate. Items stay until you delete them in the app; deleting the app deletes the library.

You can export library items as a contacts file (vCard) or spreadsheet (CSV) using the iPhone Share menu. Exports go only where you choose to send them.

Where your information goes

Contact details are saved only to the places you switch on under Save To in the app:

  • iPhone Contacts, which Apple may sync to your iCloud account according to your iPhone settings.
  • Google accounts you sign in to. The app sends each new contact directly from your iPhone to Google Contacts using the Google People API.
  • Microsoft accounts you sign in to. The app sends each new contact directly from your iPhone to Outlook / Microsoft 365 using Microsoft Graph.

Nothing passes through any server of ours. Once a contact is in one of these accounts, Apple's, Google's or Microsoft's own privacy policies apply to it.

Signing in to Google and Microsoft

You sign in on Google's or Microsoft's own sign-in page; the app never sees your password. The app asks for permission to see your account's email address (to show which account is which) and to manage contacts. The resulting access tokens are stored in your iPhone's secure Keychain, on that device only, and are deleted when you sign out of the account in the app.

The app uses this access only to: create contacts you have scanned or pasted; look up whether that person is already in the account (by email, phone, or name and company) so you can avoid duplicates; and, if you choose "Update Existing", add the new details to that existing contact. It never deletes contacts, never removes existing information, never exports your contacts, and does not access email, calendars, files or any other data. Google and Microsoft do not offer a permission limited to adding contacts, which is why their sign-in screens describe broader contact access.

Google user data

CardScan's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: information received from Google is used only to provide the contact-saving feature you asked for; it is not transferred to anyone; it is not used for advertising; it is not read by any person; and it is not used to develop or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Purchases

If the app offers an in-app purchase, payment is handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Your choices

  • Turn any destination on or off under Save To.
  • Sign out of a Google or Microsoft account in the app (press and hold it), which deletes its tokens from your iPhone. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/connections or account.live.com/consent/Manage.
  • Withdraw Camera, Photos or Contacts permission at any time in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Delete individual cards, or several at once, from the Card Library.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone, including the Card Library. Contacts already saved stay in your Contacts and accounts until you delete them there.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes
